## Reddit (EFL and lower-league fantasy communities)
I built a proper tool for the official Fantasy EFL game, because nobody else has.

The thing that convinced me it needed its own model: across 83,698 real appearances, the forward is the worst-scoring position in this game, and minutes matter more than form. Carry Premier League instincts across and you will get it wrong.

It reads the official game's own public feed, so no account and no API key. Weekly seven, captain and two club picks across the Championship, League One and League Two, with the fixture reasoning shown.

Every round's picks are committed before the deadline and graded afterwards against a random legal seven and a naive form seven. Four rounds graded so far. The whole record is public, including the rounds it got wrong.

Free to use. Happy to take questions on the model.

## LinkedIn
An observation from building a fantasy football model for the English Football League rather than the Premier League.

Across 83,698 real appearances, the forward is the worst-scoring position in the official Fantasy EFL game, and minutes played beat recent form as a predictor. Both are the opposite of Premier League intuition, and neither is obvious without measuring.

Reusing the Premier League model would have produced confident, wrong answers. So it got its own, and the record is published every round.
