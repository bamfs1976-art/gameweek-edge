DefCon, explained

Defensive Contribution points are the cheapest edge in FPL — and they're back for 2026/27.

Defenders

10
actions in a match

Clearances, blocks, interceptions & tackles. Recoveries don't count for defenders.

→ +2 pts

Midfielders & forwards

12
actions in a match

The same four, plus ball recoveries. A higher bar, but far more ways to clear it.

→ +2 pts
The cap

Hit the threshold once and that's the lot — a maximum of +2 per player, per match.

Which is why a defensive mid can be a near-nailed cheap returner.
