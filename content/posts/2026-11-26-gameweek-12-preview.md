The Gameweek 12 deadline is Friday 27 November at 18:30 UK time. The model's headline call is an unusual one: captain Van Hecke, a defender. Van Hecke tops the projections this week, and three of the five best captain options are defenders. That is what the data says, and it is worth understanding before you follow it.

## The captaincy call

**Van Hecke** (TOT, DEF, £5.2m, 20.1% owned) projects 6.8 points, the highest of any player this week. He has a 9.3% chance of a haul (10 points or more) and a 27.6% chance of a blank (2 points or fewer). His start chance is 96.1%. He started his last game and all 11 of his club's games this season.

The runner-up is **Groß** (BHA, MID, £6.3m, 49.6% owned) on 6.3 projected points, with an 11.3% haul chance, a 24.3% blank chance and a 96% start chance.

Why Van Hecke? The ranking is built on projected points, and he has the top projection. But be clear about the risk. Groß projects lower, yet he has the better haul chance and the lower blank chance. Van Hecke's edge is in the average, not the ceiling. Three defenders in the top five (Van Hecke, Hill and Vuskovic) is an unusual week, and a defender on the armband is an unusual call. The model is not hiding anything here: it simply rates these defenders highly for this one gameweek. If you prefer the higher ceiling and you own Groß, he is a sound choice too, and captaining him keeps you level with about half of managers. It is your call.
