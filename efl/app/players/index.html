Fantasy EFL player finder

Every player in the Championship, League One and League Two, ranked on the things that decide a Fantasy EFL round: recent form, starts and minutes, attacking and defensive output, and the fixture in front of them. Filter down to the seven you actually have to choose between. Fixture ratings come from the fixture ticker.

Method

On ban risk. The EFL suspends a player for accumulating bookings — five by his club's 19th league match is a one-match ban, ten by the 37th is two, fifteen at any point is three, and the count does not reset when a ban is served. A player one booking away is flagged here. It does not change his rating: a ban costs the round after the booking, not the one you are picking, so this is a planning signal rather than a mark against him. How the ladder works →

On differentials. The official game publishes ownership for clubs — you will find it in the club picker whenever the data source in use supplies it. For players nothing public publishes it, so this page shows no ownership column and does not invent one. The differential score here is an editorial, modelled measure instead: strong recent output at a club that gets less of the attention — a lower division, or further down its table. Treat it as a prompt to look, not as evidence that a player is lightly picked.
