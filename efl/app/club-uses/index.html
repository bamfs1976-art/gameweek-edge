Fantasy EFL club uses

Each club can be one of your two weekly club picks five times a season. Log the clubs you pick each round and this page keeps the count, warns before you pick a club with no uses left, and reads the fixture ticker to show which clubs are worth a use in the next three rounds and which are worth saving. How the read works

A club with uses left should spend them on its best rounds. For each club, the next twelve rounds are ranked by the same modelled fixture rating the fixture ticker uses, with a double round counted as two chances. If one of the next three rounds is among a club's best rounds for the uses it has left, it is marked worth a use now. If its best rounds all come later, it is marked worth saving. Only the twelve top-rated clubs this round are read this way; the rest are listed with their counts. Ratings are modelled from results and fixtures, normalised inside each division.

The official game is the authority on how many times you have picked a club. This log counts only what you enter, so check it against your team history in the game if the two disagree.
