Captain comparison

Any two players side by side for this round's armband. It starts with the model's captain and the next best in its seven; search to change either. The low, middle and high figures are a modelled range built from each player's scoring rate and fixtures, with the captain's points doubled. They are ratings to compare, not forecasts.

      How the range is built

      Middle: the player's points per appearance, pulled towards his position's average while his record is short, times a fixture factor for each match in the round (1.3 for the easiest fixture, 0.7 for the hardest). High: a good day by the scoring rules for his position, or twice his rate if that is higher, with the same fixture factor. Low: appearance points only, 2 for a regular starter and 1 for a likely one. A double round adds each match. Out, doubt and suspended flags scale all three. The figures are doubled for the captain.
