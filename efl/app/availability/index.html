Fantasy EFL availability

Who is out, doubtful, suspended or at risk of rotation across all 72 clubs in the Championship, League One and League Two. Each flag carries a short note, where it came from and when it was last checked. Flags show on the player finder, the dashboard picks and inside the team rater for your saved seven.

Where the flags come from

Team news. Entries kept by hand from club press conferences and announcements. A team news entry replaces what the feed says for that player until the round it is set to expire after, so a recovered player can be cleared and a fresh injury can be added before the official game catches up. An entry more than three days old is marked as possibly out of date.

Official game feed. The injury details the official Fantasy EFL game publishes for every player. The checked time is when this site last read the feed, not when the club made its announcement.

Modelled rotation risk. A player in the top quarter of his position in his own division on points per appearance, who has played between 35% and 70% of his club's league matches. This is worked out from the data, not reported by anyone, and is labelled as modelled wherever it shows.

How flags change the ratings. Out, doubt and suspended lower a player's modelled rating with the same multipliers the model already uses, so the dashboard will not pick a player this page lists as out. Rotation risk changes nothing, because the rating already counts how often a player starts. The yellow card ban ladder is separate and shows on the player finder.
