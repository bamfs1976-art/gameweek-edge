> the fixture-swing grid — a conflict that did not exist

The apparent clashSame site, GW1–GW10 grid, seven crests a week under a header reading "Easiest", numbers 2.00–2.80. Read the obvious way it says Arsenal at home to promoted Coventry is only the 6th-easiest fixture of GW1, behind Leeds away at Forest and Liverpool away at Newcastle. That contradicts our register — and it contradicts THE ODDS PAGE OF THE SAME WEBSITE, which has Arsenal 60.3% for a clean sheet, best in the league by fifteen points, against Leeds 23.8% and Liverpool 24.5%.
Which is the tellTwo pages of one site at opposite ends of the same fixture is not a finding about football. It is a signal that the READER has guessed at a definition. So the grid was checked before it was believed.
The shape of the numberAll 70 cells are multiples of 0.20 and NONE is below 2.00 — though these are the seven EASIEST of twenty each week. A single-gameweek scale would show a 1 or a 2 for the easiest fixture on the board. Averages regress to the middle, and a mean of five integers in 1–5 makes exactly 0.20 steps.
Three cells that settle itGW10 ranks Arsenal 11th while they are AT HOME TO HULL — no difficulty scale says that; their GW10–14 is HUL(H) NEW(a) MCI(H) BRE(a) TOT(a), a hard run starting easy. GW10 ranks Villa 3rd-easiest while they are AWAY AT OLD TRAFFORD — MUN(a) SUN(H) IPS(a) EVE(H) CRY(H), one hard game then four soft. GW9's best cell is Spurs at exactly 2.00 — CRY(H) LEE(a) IPS(H) SUN(a) FUL(H), five fixtures that each rate 2, and five twos average 2.00. All resolved through our own verified GW9–18 grid.
Conclusion, hedged on purposeAlmost certainly a forward-looking five-gameweek rolling average — exactly what a rotation planner should show, since you pick a team for a RUN of fixtures. Fits every case tested, contradicted by nothing. Still UNCONFIRMED: the page does not state its definition and we do not read the site directly. Stored with the hypothesis labelled as such, and no test asserts it — a guess put somewhere that goes green is a guess promoted to a fact.
Why this is written up at allIt is a NEAR MISS. One step from recording a three-way conflict between our model, the market and a fixture grid — and the whole conflict was manufactured by assuming what a column header meant. Same failure as the referee probe, the robots.txt read and the "Garcia" row, with one difference: this time the check ran first. It cost about five minutes.
