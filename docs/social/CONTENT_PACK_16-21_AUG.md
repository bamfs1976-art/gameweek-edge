## What's different, and why

**Nothing to fill in.** The old pack had `[name] — [xP] at £[x.x]m` in almost
every post, which meant opening the app, reading numbers off a panel and typing
them into a caption before you could post anything. That is the friction that
makes a pack get skipped. Here, **the card carries the numbers and the caption
carries none** — every caption below is copy-paste, verbatim, no lookups.

**One post a day, about two minutes each.** Six days, six posts. The workflow
is the same every time:

> Open the app → **Studio → Social Studio** → tap the preset → the card renders
> from today's live model → **Download** → paste the caption below.

Social Studio is owner-only and rebuilds from fresh data every time the panel
opens, so a card made on the morning you post carries that morning's prices.
That is the whole reason to render in-app rather than schedule a graphic days
early.

**You have not missed the important half.** The posts that carried the old pack
— the model's GW1 XI, the captaincy call, the deadline nudge — were always
deadline-week posts. What's gone is the warm-up: the DefCon explainer, the chip
plan, the trust post. Two of those are evergreen and slot back in whenever you
want them; none of them were the payload.
