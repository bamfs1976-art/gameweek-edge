## Why this isn't a dated plan

Two reasons, and the second is the useful one.

**I can't confirm when the break falls.** The app works it out from the gaps
between FPL deadlines (`intlBreakGws` in `index.html`), which needs the live
API. Rather than guess a date and have you build a week around it, this is
keyed to something you can always see: **the deadline**.

**Deadlines move.** A Saturday-morning deadline and a Friday-night one shift
every post by a day. Naming the days "Monday, Wednesday, Friday" would break the
first time a gameweek starts on a Friday — which it already did in GW1. So each
post below is named by its **offset from the deadline**, and the rhythm holds
whatever the fixture list does.

The pattern repeats every gameweek from here until May. Two or three turns of it
get you to the break.

## The cycle — four posts, one turn of the week

| When | Post | Image | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D−3** | The Debrief ⭐ | your own screenshot | What last week's captain cost you |
| **D−2** | Captaincy call ⭐ | Studio → **Best captains** | The decision people act on |
| **D−1** | Price watch | Price Predictor screenshot | Urgency, and a reason to open the app |
| **Matchday** | Live DefCon ⭐ | live screenshot, mid-match | The thing nobody else can post |

**D** is the deadline. For a Saturday-11:00 deadline that reads Wednesday,
Thursday, Friday, Saturday afternoon. For a Friday-night deadline it reads
Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday afternoon. Work back from the deadline
strip in the app and it lands right every time.

**If the week gets away from you, do two: D−2 and matchday.** The captaincy
call is what people act on, and the live DefCon post is the only one in the
whole cycle a competitor physically cannot make. Everything else is upside.

## What changes at the break

Very little, and that is the point — the cycle just pauses. Two weeks with no
deadline means no captaincy call and no price panic, so use the gap for the
posts that never fit during a normal week:

- **The season so far, graded.** The model's record over the opening gameweeks,
  published whichever way it falls. The break is the only time anyone reads a
  longer post.
- **The chip plan** for the rest of the half — Studio → **Chip plan**. Nobody
  plans chips during a normal week; everybody has time during a break.
- **DefCon explainer** (`assets/social/gwe-defcon-explainer.png`) if you never
  got to it — still evergreen, still nobody else's angle.

Then the cycle restarts at D−3 of the first gameweek back.
