## 4. Live-posting rules

**Post the misses.** The single fastest way to burn the credibility built on
11 Aug is to publish only the hits. P19 and P24 both have a "biggest miss" line
and it is not optional. A model that publishes its bad days is a model; one that
doesn't is a tipster with a spreadsheet.

**Never post a number you haven't seen on screen.** Every `[bracket]` this
weekend is checkable against a live match by everybody reading it. One invented
figure and the trust post from 11 Aug becomes a liability.

**Check before you claim.** Before any grading post, confirm the app's bonus and
DefCon figures against the official source. If they diverge, you fix the product
first and post second — the divergence itself is not a story you want.

**Reactive quote-posts, sparingly.** Two or three across the weekend, no more.
The three that are worth it:
- A DefCon threshold hit live, quote-posting a big account complaining they
  can't tell whether a defender got there.
- A bonus finalisation that matched the live projection exactly.
- A goal from a player the model flagged as underpriced on 7 Aug — quote your
  own post from that day. Receipts.

**Don't live-post during a match you're actually watching.** P18 is the
exception and it's worth interrupting yourself for. Everything else can wait for
half-time or full time.

**Reply more than you post.** The weekend's real work is answering people. Every
"what did my captain cost me?" reply is a live demo of P23, one manager at a
time, and those convert far better than broadcasts.

## 5. Contingencies

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| **The FPL API lags or falls over mid-match** | Say so plainly, once, and don't post numbers until it's back. "Official feed is lagging — holding off on the live figures until it catches up" costs nothing. Silently posting stale numbers costs everything |
| **Bonus projection diverges from official** | Don't post P16. Fix, then post P19 with the corrected figures and a line acknowledging it. Own it before someone else finds it |
| **The model has a genuinely bad day** | Post P19 and P24 anyway, unchanged. Lead with the miss rather than burying it. This is the single highest-trust move available to you all season, and it's only available on a bad day |
| **A post takes off** | Reply to the first thirty people properly, from the app. Then — and only then — quote-post it with one extra number. Don't pin it over Post 7 |
| **You get the weekend wrong and miss a slot** | Skip it. Don't post P17 at 19:00 or P23 at 4pm. A late post in a live window reads as automation, which is the exact opposite of the brand |
| **Nothing lands at all** | Fine. The weekend's job is proving the product works, not going viral. The screenshots, the graded scorecard and the debrief all still exist for the GW2 pack |

## 7. What to capture while it's happening

You'll never get a first weekend again — so instrument it. Note these down as
you go; they set the whole GW2 pack and every launch post afterwards.

- **Screenshots of anything visually good**, especially mid-match DefCon and
  bonus. These become Social Studio cards and app-store screenshots.
- **Every question people ask in replies.** Repeated questions are your FAQ,
  your next explainer post, and usually a missing bit of onboarding.
- **Which post drove sign-ups**, not which got likes. Check the Analytics panel
  Tuesday morning; the two are rarely the same post.
- **Anything that broke**, with the time and the panel. Friday evening is the
  most-watched hour the product will have had.
- **The model's actual GW1 numbers**, kept for the season-long ledger. GW1 is
  data point one of thirty-eight.
