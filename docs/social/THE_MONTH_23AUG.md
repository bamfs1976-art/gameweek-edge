# Gameweek Edge — the month

### Sun 23 Aug → Sun 20 Sep 2026 · **X + Bluesky**

> **The app now carries this.** Studio → Social Studio opens on a **Posting
> order** — every card in the queue with its date and time, worked out from
> the real deadlines, and the gallery sorted to match. That is the thing to
> follow day to day; it cannot go stale when a deadline moves, and this
> document can.
>
> What is kept here is the part the app does not schedule: the Debrief, the
> live DefCon shot, the break, and the reasoning behind the shape.

Replaces the caption-carrying packs. It carries **no captions at all**, on
purpose — they now live inside the app, next to the card they belong to.

---

## What changed

The old packs failed for one reason: the picture was in the app and the words
were in a file. Posting meant having both open, on a phone, at nine at night.

So the words moved. Every card in **Studio → Social Studio** now carries its
own caption underneath it:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Share** | Hands the picture *and* the caption to X or Bluesky together — the post is already written when the app opens |
| **Copy for X** | The X version, no hashtag, inside 280 characters |
| **Bluesky** | The Bluesky version, `#FPL` included, inside 300 characters |
| **Alt text** | The image description — paste into the alt field before posting |

**The GW Debrief is in the queue too**, under *From your own screen*. It has no
card because the picture is a screenshot of your own debrief panel — but it
carries the same three copy buttons, and it appears in the posting order with
its date like everything else.

**Share is the whole workflow.** Tap the card's Share button, pick X or
Bluesky, post. On a phone that is three taps and no typing. Copy for X is the
fallback for desktop, where the share sheet doesn't exist.

The caption shown under each card is the Bluesky one, because it's the shorter
of the two and reads as a finished post either way. What you see is what
you'd send.

Both versions are written for a **standard account with no Premium
subscription**. X is the tighter of the two: it bills every link at a flat 23
characters however short it is, and counts each emoji as two, so 280 buys
rather less than it looks like it does. Every caption is checked against that
arithmetic in CI, so nothing here can quietly grow past the limit again.

## The shape of the month

Four turns of the same four-post week. **Two of the four are the ones that
matter**; the other two are upside on a week when you have the time.

| | Post | Where it comes from | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D−5** | The Debrief ⭐ | your own GW Debrief screen | Highest-converting post you have |
| **D−3** | Something useful | a Studio card, rotating | Utility, and a reason to open the app |
| **D−2** | Captaincy call ⭐ | Studio → **Best captains** | **The one people act on** |
| **D−1** | Something useful | a Studio card, rotating | Utility again |
| **Matchday** | Live DefCon ⭐ | live screenshot, mid-match | **Nobody else can post this** |

The Debrief sits five days out, which is the Monday of an ordinary
Saturday-deadline week — the gameweek just gone is still raw and nobody has
started thinking about the next one. It's in the app's queue like everything
else; the app just asks you for the screenshot instead of drawing a card.

**D is the deadline.** Every date below is my best read of the fixture
calendar, but the app's deadline strip is the thing that's actually right —
if a date here disagrees with the app, the app wins and you shift by a day.

## Times

| | X | Bluesky |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekday evenings** | 18:30–20:00 | 20:00–21:30 |
| **Sunday** | 19:00–20:30 | 19:30–21:00 |
| **Matchday** | mid-15:00 block, live | same |

Bluesky runs later and timing matters more there — its feeds are close to
chronological, so a mistimed post is lost rather than merely delayed. The later
slot also catches the US afternoon.

Hashtags: `#FPL` on Bluesky, none on X. Already handled — the two Copy buttons
give you the right one for each platform.

These are reasonable defaults for a UK-majority audience, not measurements.
Your own Analytics panel will beat them inside a month.

## When the week gets away from you

It will. **Do the two starred posts and let the rest go**: the captaincy call,
because it's the decision people act on, and the live DefCon post, because it
is the only thing in this document a competitor physically cannot make.

Two posts a week, both under a minute now that the caption travels with the
card. That's the floor, and the floor is enough.
